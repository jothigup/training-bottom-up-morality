# Supplied Zenodo record 13957177: dataset-fit assessment

The supplied `/Users/jothigupta/Downloads/13957177.json` is a Zenodo **metadata record**,
not the dataset rows. A provenance copy is stored in
`data/raw/agoraspeech/zenodo_record_13957177.json`. The record links to `AgoraSpeech.csv`.

According to the [official record](https://zenodo.org/records/13957177), AgoraSpeech
covers Greek political campaign speeches with paragraph-level topic, sentiment,
polarization, populism, agenda/criticism, and named-entity annotations, initially
generated by ChatGPT and human-validated. The metadata lists CC BY 4.0.

Those are not ethical-behavior labels. Negative sentiment, polarization, or populism
must not be mapped to “immoral”; positive sentiment must not be mapped to “moral”.
Political opinions and criticism likewise do not determine ethical conduct.

Decision: **not used as the primary moral evaluator training set or answer-key benchmark**.
No source labels are converted, and the CSV has not been downloaded. It could later
support a separately framed political-domain transfer experiment, but that requires
new task-specific human ethical labels, topic screening, and grouping all paragraphs
from the same speech together. It would expand the present project's scope.

The active workflow instead uses generator-disjoint evaluator reserves plus original
final prompts. The user can provide another scenario/response dataset; inspect label
meaning and provenance before adding it, preserve source IDs, group related scenarios,
and never use final-test rows to train the generators or BERT classifier.
